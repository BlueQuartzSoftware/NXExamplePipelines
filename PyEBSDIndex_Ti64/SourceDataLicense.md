# Source data attribution

David Rowenhorst and Patrick Callahan, with contributor Håkon Ånes, released [PyEBSDIndex Demo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8400425) on October 2, 2023, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit the authors and the US Naval Research Laboratory dataset source.

The example uses a measured-derived 32×32 acquisition crop. Only scan row/column counts in the 42-byte UP1 header change; every selected detector value is retained. The two-pattern preview comes from DREAM3D-NX output. Source data, the notebook, and the paper are not redistributed here. Keep this attribution and identify the crop when distributing the prepared input or derived results.

`DataSources.json` records the transformation and input hashes. Its verification flags distinguish the checked crop from the complete archive, whose full checksum was not verified.
